## Abstract

We examine the implications of virus mutation for optimal lockdown policy in an epimacro model. We consider one deterministic specification and two stochastic specifications with two-state and three-state Markov processes. The effects of mutation are asymmetric: a future reduction in the transmission rate increases optimal lockdown intensity by more than a future rise in the transmission rate lowers it. Consequently, greater uncertainty about future mutation is non-neutral and reduces lockdown intensity under the optimal policy.
